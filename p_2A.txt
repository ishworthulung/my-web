I built a small model of a traffic light that
works like a real one. I used an Arduino microcontroller
to control LEDs and a seven-segment display.

I programmed it so that the red LED lights up first, 
while the display shows a countdown timer. After the timer 
ends, the light switches to yellow for a few seconds, and 
then to green. When the green LED is on, the display shows "GO."

The components I used include an Arduino, LEDs, resistors, 
a seven-segment display, and connecting wires.
