This project is a pop-up LED light hidden inside a box. 
When the switch is turned on, the sliding part comes out 
and the LED glows. To turn it off, the switch is flipped 
again and the slider retracts back into the box, hiding the light.

• Inspiration & Build
I built this project about 5–6 years ago, at a time when I didn’t 
yet know about Arduino, microcontrollers, or even transistors.
Instead, I used simple aluminum foil pieces as switches to complete the circuit.
The sliding mechanism was repurposed from an old DVD player’s CD tray, which 
moves in and out when inserting or ejecting a disc.
I attached LEDs to the slider, so when it extended, the aluminum foil touched another foil connected with wires, completing the circuit and lighting up the LEDs.
