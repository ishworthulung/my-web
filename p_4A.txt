Overview
This project demonstrates how lights can be turned on automatically 
without pressing a switch. The system detects the presence of a person 
using an IR sensor module and then turns on LEDs step by step. 
After a short delay, the LEDs turn off again in sequence.

For the demo, I used an IR sensor module. Its limitation is that 
it detects any object in front of the sensor, not just humans. 
In real-life applications, more advanced sensors (like PIR sensors) can 
be used to detect only human presence.
