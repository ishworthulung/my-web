• Features
A side switch allows you to toggle between 
decimal-to-binary and binary-to-decimal conversion modes.
Red and blue LEDs indicate the current conversion status
(red for decimal-to-binary, blue for binary-to-decimal).
Three push buttons are included:
One to increase the binary/decimal value
One for "OK"
One for reset
Powered by a 3.7V rechargeable battery with a charging module, making the device portable and easy to carry anywhere.
