
> Components Used
• Arduino Nano – Acts as the CPU of the car, 
controlling all functions such as movement and lights.

• Motor Driver – Since the Arduino cannot supply 
enough current to run motors directly, the motor 
driver handles motor operations.

• Bluetooth Module – Connects with the mobile phone 
via Bluetooth, receives transmitted data, decodes it, and sends it to the Arduino in a readable format.

• Siren – Works as the car’s horn.

• LEDs – For headlights, side lights, and back lights.

• Motors – Drive the car forward and backward.
