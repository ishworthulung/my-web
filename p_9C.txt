
-> What We Learn
• Servo vs. Regular Motors: Unlike regular motors that spin continuously when powered, servo motors allow precise control over rotation angles (e.g., 90°, 180°). This makes them essential in robotics where accuracy is critical.

• Handling Servo Motors: Through this project, we gain practical experience in programming and controlling servo motors for accurate positioning.

• Understanding LDRs: By experimenting with LDRs, we learn how changes in resistance (lower ohms vs. higher ohms) affect electronic circuits depending on light intensity.
