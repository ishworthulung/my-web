This project is great for anyone starting out in robotics and electronics. 
It helps you understand how a motor, battery, and switch work together.

You’ll also learn about the effect of battery polarity:
• If the battery is connected in reverse, the car runs 
    backward.
• Flip the battery to the correct orientation, and the
    car runs forward.

It’s a fun and practical way to see how electricity powers motion.
