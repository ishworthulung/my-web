• Components Used
→ Arduino Uno: Controls the LEDs and processes sensor input.
→ IR Sensor Module: Detects the presence of objects.
→ LEDs: Used to simulate automatic lighting.
→ Jumper Wires: For connections.
→ Cardboard: As a base for mounting components.

• How It Works
The IR sensor continuously monitors for objects.
When it detects presence, the Arduino turns on LEDs one by one with a delay.
After a short wait, the LEDs turn off step by step.
This creates a visually appealing effect, similar to traffic lights or decorative lighting.
